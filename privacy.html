Privacy policy

How CRRT Calculators handles data on this device and optional backend.

What we store

Saved calculator inputs/results and optional labels. No account system. Data stays in your browser localStorage and, when the API is running, in a local SQLite database on the server host.

What we do not collect

We do not sell data, run advertising trackers, or require patient identifiers. Avoid entering PHI (names, MRNs, DOB). Use bed labels only if your policy allows.

HTTPS

Pages request HTTPS via Content-Security-Policy upgrade-insecure-requests. Local development may use http://127.0.0.1 for the Vite app (port 5174) and API (port 3851).

Your rights

Export a JSON copy of your data, or permanently delete everything from this browser and the backend database.

This tool is for educational / clinical reference. Not a medical device. Not for store submission claims of diagnosis or treatment.
